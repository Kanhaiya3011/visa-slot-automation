Privacy Policy for Visa Slot Guard

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Visa Slot Guard browser extension (the "Extension"). We prioritize your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent data practices.

Key Summary: Visa Slot Guard operates entirely within your local browser. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data, login credentials, or security responses to any external servers operated by us or third parties.

1. Overview & Single Purpose

Visa Slot Guard is designed with the sole purpose of monitoring usvisascheduling.com for visa appointment availability, alerting users when slots become open, and assisting in managing session resumptions locally.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We adhere strictly to Chrome Web Store User Data Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Local Storage: User preferences, session states, and appointment search criteria are saved exclusively on your local device using the Chrome chrome.storage API.
  • Authentication & Form Data: Any session tokens, cookie states, or auto-fill configurations remain entirely within your browser runtime. No credentials or personal inputs are transmitted to external servers.
  • Network Checks: The extension queries https://api.ipify.org exclusively to verify public IP connectivity for network status checks. No personal identifiers are attached to this request.

3. Use of Permissions

The Extension requests specific browser permissions strictly to perform its core single purpose:

  • storage: To locally store user settings and notification options.
  • notifications: To alert you when visa appointment slots are detected.
  • alarms: To schedule routine local background checks for availability.
  • tabs and scripting: To interact with and inspect usvisascheduling.com DOM elements while actively in use.
  • cookies: To detect and respond to portal session timeouts locally.
  • Host Permissions (usvisascheduling.com, b2clogin.com): To execute client-side scripts solely on the relevant visa scheduling portal domains.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We explicitly state that:

  • User data is never sold to third parties under any circumstances.
  • User data is not transferred or used for reasons outside the extension's core functionality.
  • User data is never used to determine creditworthiness or for financial evaluation.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or extension updates. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the official support channel or repository where you downloaded the extension.
